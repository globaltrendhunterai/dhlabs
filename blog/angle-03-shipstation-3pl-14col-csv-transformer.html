Frequently Asked Technical Questions

What are the exact 14 columns generated by AF-GL-ECOM-03?

The standardized columns are: (1) OrderNumber, (2) RecipientName, (3) RecipientPhone, (4) Email, (5) PostalCode, (6) StreetAddress, (7) SkuBundle, (8) Quantity, (9) WeightOz, (10) CarrierCode, (11) CustomsHsCode, (12) WarehouseCode, (13) Incoterms, and (14) HouseholdMergeKey.

How does the workflow prevent duplicate order submissions to ShipStation?

The workflow incorporates an Idempotency Dedup Guard backed by the local engine cache. Once an order number is verified and injected into ShipStation, subsequent polling sweeps skip the record automatically.

Yes. Because the output is fully standardized, you can direct the output node to export CSV files directly to an SFTP server, Google Drive, or local storage for direct warehouse consumption.
