Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Why is 1-Cent ($0.01) precision necessary for e-commerce stores?

Processing fees often include subtle tier shifts, international card surcharges (e.g. +1.5%), and FX markups. A 1-cent auditor catches systematic misconfigurations across tens of thousands of orders before they compound into major losses.

How does the workflow handle multi-currency conversions (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY)?

The workflow captures original gross currency and gateway payout currency, calculating exact FX conversion spreads against baseline rates to isolate currency conversion slippage from transaction fees.

Does AF-GL-FIN-05 generate audit trails for corporate accountants and CPAs?

Yes. Every transaction is recorded with transaction ID, order reference, gross, calculated fee, actual fee, difference, and timestamp in an audit-ready format compatible with QuickBooks, Xero, and Excel.
