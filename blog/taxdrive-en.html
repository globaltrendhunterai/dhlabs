Q1. What does the IRS require for a valid Schedule C vehicle mileage log?

Under IRS Publication 463 and Form 4562 Part V, taxpayers claiming the standard mileage deduction must maintain contemporaneous records showing the date of each trip, business destination, business purpose, trip distance, and total beginning/ending odometer readings for the tax year.
