Why does WhisperX fail with "float16 compute type not supported" or pyannote 401 errors?

Yes. Once the WhisperX and pyannote models are cached on your disk, 100% of speech-to-text transcription and speaker diarization (Speaker 01, Speaker 02) runs locally on your machine with zero cloud uploads.
